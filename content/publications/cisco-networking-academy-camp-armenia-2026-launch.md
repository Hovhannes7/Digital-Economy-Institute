The **Institute of Digital Economy (IoDE)** is pleased to announce the launch of **Cisco Networking Academy Camp Armenia 2026**, a structured learning initiative designed to strengthen digital and professional skills in Armenia.

The camp is being implemented within the framework of the Memorandum of Understanding between [**Cisco Networking Academy**](https://www.linkedin.com/school/cisco-networking-academy1/) and the [**Ministry of High-Tech Industry of the Republic of Armenia**](https://www.linkedin.com/company/htiarm/).

The program officially opens on **4 August 2026** with a ceremony bringing together representatives of the Ministry of High-Tech Industry of the Republic of Armenia, Cisco Networking Academy, IoDE, and BAU Academy. The opening event includes welcome remarks, thematic lectures, interactive group activities, networking, and an introduction to the learning program.

During August and September, participants will follow a structured program covering five learning areas:

1. **Digital Awareness**
2. **Introduction to Cybersecurity**
3. **The Art of Investigation**
4. **Introduction to Modern AI**
5. **Update Your Resume with AI**

The initiative is designed to help participants strengthen their **digital literacy, cybersecurity awareness, practical AI skills, and professional readiness**.

The learning process combines Cisco Networking Academy courses with practical sessions, assessments, learner support, and interactive activities. Participants who successfully complete the required learning activities and assessments will be eligible for the applicable Cisco Networking Academy certificate and/or digital badge, depending on the completion requirements of each course.

Through this initiative, IoDE continues its work to connect Armenian institutions and professionals with international organizations, introduce new educational approaches and opportunities, and expand access to internationally recognized technology education.

Cisco Networking Academy Camp Armenia 2026 is intended to contribute to the development of digital capabilities and professional skills while creating further opportunities for learning and cooperation.
