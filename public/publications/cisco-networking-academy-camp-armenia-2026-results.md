**Cisco Networking Academy Camp Armenia 2026** concluded on **25 September 2026**, completing a learning initiative launched within the framework of the Memorandum of Understanding between the **Ministry of High-Tech Industry of the Republic of Armenia** and **Cisco Networking Academy**.

The program provided free learning opportunities across five areas:

1. **Digital Awareness**
2. **Introduction to Cybersecurity**
3. **The Art of Investigation**
4. **Introduction to Modern AI**
5. **Update Your Resume with AI**

Across these learning areas, **396 learners participated in the program**.

Of them, **130 participants successfully completed the examination stage and received certificates**.

The courses introduced participants to practical topics related to digital literacy, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, investigation skills, and professional development, helping learners strengthen competencies increasingly relevant to education and the modern workplace.

The initiative was **organized and coordinated by the Institute of Digital Economy Foundation (IoDE)** with the support of **BAU BRANDING**.

Cisco Networking Academy Camp Armenia 2026 was designed not only as a series of individual courses, but also as an opportunity to broaden access to structured technology education and strengthen cooperation around digital-skills development in Armenia.

Given the successful implementation and results of the 2026 program, the training initiative is **planned to continue in 2027**.

The 2026 camp therefore represents a foundation for further cooperation, expanded learning opportunities, and continued efforts to strengthen digital and professional capabilities in Armenia.
